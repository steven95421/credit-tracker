Data Deletion Instructions

Updated August 29, 2026

Disconnect one linked account

  1. Sign in to Credit Tracker with the approved Google account.
  2. Open Cards & Accounts.
  3. Select Unlink on the relevant provider item and confirm.

This disconnects the account from the provider and deletes the associated local account metadata and transactions. A card profile can remain for manual tracking until you remove it separately.

Delete manual data

Remove the relevant card in Cards & Accounts. Removing a card deletes its card-to-product mapping and benefit-use adjustments. Unlink the provider item separately if a connected account is still present.

Request complete deletion

If you cannot sign in or want all data associated with your approved account removed, open a support request using the support page with the words Data deletion request. Include only the Google email domain or GitHub username needed to arrange private verification—never post an account number, transaction, access token, password, or financial-institution credential. After identity is verified privately, the operator will complete the request within 30 days unless retention is required by law.
