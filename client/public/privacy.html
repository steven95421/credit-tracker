💳 Credit Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

Credit Tracker is an independent, private-beta tool for monitoring recurring credit-card statement credits. This policy explains the data the service processes, why it is used, and how an approved user can delete it.

Data we process

  • Authentication: the Google email address and identity token used to verify that an account is approved. We do not receive or store a Google password.
  • Connected-account metadata: provider and institution identifiers, account name, type, and masked account digits.
  • Transaction data: transaction date, description, amount, currency, pending status, provider identifiers, and the provider's original transaction record made available through a connected account.
  • Data you add: CSV transaction rows, card-product mappings, display names, and manual benefit-use adjustments.
  • Technical data: limited request and error information processed by the hosting providers to operate and secure the service.
Financial Connections scope. The Stripe connection requests Transactions permission and limits selection to credit-card accounts. Credit Tracker does not request balances, ownership data, ACH account and routing numbers, payment-method access, payments, or transfers. Bank login credentials are handled by Stripe and the financial institution, not by Credit Tracker.

How we use data

Data is used only to authenticate approved users; link, refresh, and display eligible accounts; match transaction descriptions to configured statement-credit benefits; show remaining or expiring benefits; process unlink requests; and protect and troubleshoot the service. Credit Tracker does not sell personal or financial data and does not use it for advertising or credit decisions.

Service providers

Stripe processes Financial Connections sessions and connected-account data; Google provides sign-in; Cloudflare hosts the API and database; and GitHub Pages serves the public web application. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. A legacy Plaid or Teller connection is processed by that provider only when the operator has explicitly enabled it and the user chooses it.

Storage and retention

Connected-account metadata and imported transactions are stored in the operator's Cloudflare D1 database for as long as the account remains linked or the data is needed for the dashboard. Unlinking removes the corresponding local accounts and transactions and asks the provider to disconnect the account. Manual cards, CSV rows, and benefit adjustments remain until they are removed or a complete deletion is requested. Security and operational records may remain for the limited periods used by the hosting providers.

Security and access

The dashboard is restricted to approved Google accounts. Provider secret keys and webhook signing secrets are kept in server-side secret storage and are not placed in the browser or database. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should report suspected issues promptly and avoid sending sensitive financial details through public support channels.

Your choices

An approved user can unlink a connected institution and remove manual cards from the dashboard. See the Data Deletion Instructions to request deletion of all associated data. Access can be withdrawn by the operator at any time.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, use the support page; do not include account numbers, transactions, credentials, or other sensitive data in a public message.
