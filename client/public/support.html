Support

Independent private beta

For product questions, access problems, privacy requests, or data deletion, contact the Credit Tracker operator through the project's GitHub issue tracker.

Keep public messages non-sensitive. Do not include account or routing numbers, card numbers, transaction details, provider tokens, passwords, screenshots of financial data, or government identifiers. For an access or deletion request, provide only enough information to arrange private identity verification.

When reporting a problem

Include the affected screen, provider name, approximate time, and the visible error text after removing all personal and financial details. Credit Tracker cannot resolve bank coverage or Stripe account-approval decisions.
